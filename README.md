### Hi, I build small, fast, ad-free websites and trackers.

No ads and no sign-ups: static pages on GitHub Pages running on free public data (plus one small Cloudflare Worker), and one native Mac app. Most keep anonymous visit counts or cookieless analytics, and say so on the page.

**Sports trackers**

- 🏆 [Tennis Grand Slam Tracker](https://danpune.github.io/tennis-slams-tracker/) — all four majors: every draw and bracket, official highlights, rankings & countdowns, with match times in your timezone
- 🦁 [Premier League Tracker](https://danpune.github.io/epl-tracker/) — the table, all 380 fixtures plus cup ties, live scores, lineups & highlights, with every kickoff in your timezone
- 🐉 [Wrexham Tracker](https://danpune.github.io/wrexham-tracker/) — Wrexham AFC in the Championship: fixtures in your timezone, the table, match stats, promotion projection, news & podcasts
- 🏏 [India Cricket Tracker](https://danpune.github.io/india-cricket-tracker/) — Team India men's & women's live scores, fixtures with calendar feeds, full scorecards, ICC rankings & results
- ⚽ [World Cup 2026](https://danpune.github.io/worldcup2026/) — the finished tournament's archive: every result, the full knockout bracket, official highlights & 96 years of World Cup history

**Immigration**

- 🛂 [US Work Visa Tracker](https://danpune.github.io/visa-tracker/) — who sponsors H-1B & L-1 visas, a county map of where H-1B jobs are and what they pay, green card wait charts & a plain-English visa guide
- 🪪 [Green Card Next Steps Checklist](https://danpune.github.io/greencard-checklist/) — what to do after your green card is approved: IDs, work, travel rules, taxes & the road to citizenship, with a citizenship filing-date calculator and daily USCIS alerts

**Tools**

- 🌍 [Time Zones](https://danpune.github.io/timezones/) — compare cities at a glance, with real sunrise, sunset & daylight, weather and the hours everyone is awake; works offline
- 🖥️ [Time Zones for Mac](https://github.com/danpune/timezones-mac) — your cities in the Mac menu bar, coloured by their sky, with weather and the hours everyone is awake; free, and it updates itself
- 🧘 [Pranayama Guide](https://danpune.github.io/pranayama-guide/) — a short illustrated intro to pranayama: the four stages of a breath, box breathing and links to three beginner videos

The code for every project is public in [my repositories](https://github.com/danpune?tab=repositories). Feedback and PRs welcome — open an issue on any of them.
